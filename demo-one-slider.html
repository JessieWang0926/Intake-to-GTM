01
Business Problem & Complications
  • Requirements gathering is manual, inconsistent, and consumes 30% of project timelines, delaying time-to-market for critical initiatives
  • 64% of failed projects trace back to errors introduced during requirements and design phases, driving costly rework downstream
  • Fixing requirements defects post-development costs 15x more than catching them early, eroding project margins at scale
  • Institutional knowledge lives in individual heads — when BAs leave, context is lost, creating single points of failure across programs
  • Existing tools (Jira, Confluence) store artifacts but don't generate or validate them, leaving the hardest work entirely manual
02
AI Solution & Key Features
  • Custom-built AI engine generates complete, structured epics, features, user stories, and test cases from high-level business intent
  • Deploys directly into your environment — integrates with existing SDLC toolchains with zero disruption to current workflows
  • Fully tailored to your organization's standards, templates, and domain-specific terminology for Financial Services regulatory context
  • Built-in intelligent scoping and impact analysis identifies upstream and downstream dependencies before a single line of code is written
  • Trained on a curated corpus of relevant regulatory and industry documentation, providing consistent institutional memory at scale

Impact & Outcomes
  • Up to 45% reduction in manual BA effort, freeing senior analysts to focus on stakeholder alignment and strategic decisions
  • Estimated 19.5% total cost savings per project through fewer defects, less rework, and compressed requirements cycles
  • Requirements are accurate, complete, and consistent — eliminating the variability that comes with different analysts on different days
  • From kickoff to live pilot in 7 weeks — fast enough to prove value within a single quarter without a multi-year transformation
  • Scalable across teams and programs without proportional headcount growth, enabling enterprise-wide standardization of BA output quality
