Business Problem
  • Requirements are the #1 bottleneck in SDLC — 30% of tech projects are delayed by upstream specification gaps

  • Rework costs 15x more when defects originating in requirements are caught in later phases
  • Manual effort is unsustainable — BAs spend the majority of time on low-value formatting and consistency checks
  • No scalable alternative exists — off-the-shelf tools lack domain context for financial services regulatory requirements
✨
AI Solution & Features
  • BA Genie automates generation of comprehensive epics, features, stories, and test cases from high-level inputs
  • Custom-built AI tailored to client environment — trained on 6 corpuses of relevant regulatory and standards documents
  • Aligned to internal standards — outputs conform to the client's templates, taxonomy, and acceptance criteria formats
  • Model + tech agnostic — architecture supports swapping underlying models as capabilities evolve
  • Deployed in 2 weeks with no disruption to existing delivery pipelines — runs in parallel to current workflows

Impact
  • Up to 45% reduction in manual effort for requirements authoring — equivalent to ~19.5% total cost saving
  • 1.5x throughput increase — teams produce more complete, higher-quality requirements in less time
  • Higher accuracy & consistency — AI-generated outputs are accurate, complete, concise, and relevant from first draft
  • BAs shift to high-value work — freed from formatting, they focus on stakeholder engagement and edge-case analysis
