  • 64% of failed projects trace root cause to errors in requirements gathering and design phases
  • Rework costs 15x more when defects from requirements surface in testing or production
  • BAs spend most time on low-value tasks — formatting, consistency checks, and re-writing
  • No scalable alternative — off-the-shelf tools lack financial services domain and regulatory context

AI Solution & Features
  • Automates generation of epics, features, user stories, and test cases from high-level inputs
  • Custom-built AI trained on 6 corpuses of regulatory and standards documents specific to client
  • Outputs conform to internal standards — templates, taxonomy, and acceptance criteria formats
  • Model & tech agnostic architecture — swap underlying LLMs as capabilities evolve
