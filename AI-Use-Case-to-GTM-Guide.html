Global GenAI Lab

From AI Use Case Intake to Go-To-Market

A step-by-step guide for Domain Partners, AI Champions, and AI Lab teams

1
Identify & Generate AI Use Cases
Ideation

What to do

  • Talk to your Practice Lead about client pain points and market gaps
  • Each domain partner elects an AI Champion to drive use case ideation
  • Identify pain points from in-flight projects, client conversations, and market demand
  • Check the Global Demo Catalog to avoid duplicating existing solutions

Requirements

  • Nominated AI Champion per practice area
  • Clear understanding of target client personas
  • Awareness of existing 79+ solutions across 10 domains
  • Use cases must be repeatable across clients

Key roles

Practice Lead AI Champion Domain Partner Account Partner
▼
2
Submit Use Case Intake
Intake

What to do

  • Complete the AI Use-Case Intake Form (one row per use case)
  • Answer the 4 Core Desirability Questions:
    1. What is the pain/problem?
    2. Who feels the pain?
    3. Why does it matter?
    4. Why is it unsolved today?
  • Assign priority ranking: High / Medium / Low

Requirements

  • Use Case Title, Practice & Sub-Practice
  • Description, pain point, and current blocker
  • Target user persona and expected business value
  • Revenue potential and targeted accounts
  • Market demand assessment

Key roles

AI Champion AI Infusion Champion
▼
3
AI Lab Management Review
Assessment

What to do

  • Leadership determines project scope: POC, MVP, or Full Build
  • Evaluate based on Business Value and Technology Depth
  • Assess strategic alignment, data maturity, and regulatory impact
  • Prioritize top 1-2 use cases per champion for development

Requirements

  • Strategic alignment with organizational goals
  • Data readiness and maturity assessment
  • Regulatory / compliance impact analysis
  • Resource availability confirmation
  • Business sponsor sign-off

Key roles

AI Lab Leadership Business Sponsor Technology Owner
Release
Gate
Gateway: Approved to proceed? → Yes: continue  |  No: return to ideation

4
Project Setup & Resource Allocation
Setup

What to do (in parallel)

  • Asset Register: Model AI solution into the asset register and assign resources
  • Set Up Jira: Create Jira project, stories, and sprint backlog
  • Allocate Resources: Assign AI Forward Engineers and team members via Retain

Requirements

  • Jira project board with defined epics and stories
  • Assigned AI Forward Engineer(s)
  • Asset registration completed
  • Resource allocation confirmed in Retain
  • Point of contact and business sponsor identified

Key roles

AI Lab Management AI Forward Engineer Program Manager
▼
5
Define the MVP & Build
Build

What to do

  • Define MVP: Jessie and the Business define the Minimum Viable Product
  • Develop the solution through sprint cycles
  • Build demo environment with real-world scenarios
  • Conduct iterative testing and user feedback
  • Track status: Backlog → Build In-Progress → MVP Demo Complete

Requirements

  • MVP scope definition signed off by business
  • Development environment and tooling ready
  • Sprint cadence with weekly checkpoints
  • Demo script and test scenarios prepared
  • Compliance and data security review initiated

Key roles

AI Forward Engineer Business Sponsor AI Champion

6
GTM Ready: Package the Solution
GTM Ready

What to do

  • Finalize the demo script and run-through
  • Prepare the "AI vs. Non-AI" talk track (competitive differentiation)
  • Create sales enablement materials: one-pager, pricing template
  • Identify best-fit buyers and target accounts
  • Register in Global Demo Catalog with domain mapping

Deliverables

  • Completed demo with polished UX
  • One-pager / solution brief for sellers
  • Competitive talk track (1-2 lines of AI differentiation)
  • Pricing template and engagement model
  • Defined target accounts and buyer personas
  • Backlog reference and asset links

Key roles

AI Champion Domain Leader Sales Team
▼
7
GTM Push: Showcase & Sales Activation
Go-To-Market

What to do

  • Schedule AI Capability Showcases (bi-weekly rotation by domain leader)
  • Run Sales Activations targeting specific accounts
  • Form Sprint Pods for active pursuit opportunities
  • Present to clients and demonstrate the solution
  • Assign follow-up actions with owners and due dates

Requirements

  • Showcase facilitator (rotating Domain Leader)
  • Presenter and demo environment ready
  • Target accounts and sellers identified
  • Sprint pod members assigned for pursuit
  • Feedback loop back to intake for next iteration

Key roles

Domain Leader Account Partner AI Champion Business Sponsor
▼
8
Track Results & Continuous Improvement
Measure

What to do

  • Track CTAR results and revenue metrics per solution
  • Monitor demo maturity: Idea → In Build → Demo-Ready → GTM-Ready → In Pursuit
  • Capture client feedback and feature requests
  • Feed learnings back into next intake cycle

Requirements

  • Showcase tracker updated with outcomes
  • Domain metrics dashboard maintained
  • Revenue and pipeline attribution tracked
  • Continuous feedback loop to AI Champions

Key roles

Program Manager AI Lab Leadership Domain Leader
